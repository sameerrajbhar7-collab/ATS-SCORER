                How it Works?

                The ATS Resume Analyzer is designed to help job seekers audit their resumes against real-world Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and optimize their chances of landing interviews.

                1. Provide context: Paste the target Job Description in Box 1.
                2. Upload resume: Drag and drop your resume file (.pdf or .docx) in Box 2.
                3. Run matching AI: Click "Analyze Resume". The system extracts the text, performs semantic matching with OpenAI, categories your skill sets, analyzes keywords, and offers specific optimization tips.
